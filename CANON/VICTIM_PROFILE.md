# Victim Profile

## Basic Information

**Full Name:** Natasha "Tasha" Volkov
**Nickname/Handle:** @TashaVBeauty
**Age:** 24
**Nationality:** Russian-Canadian (born Moscow, raised Toronto)
**Occupation:** Beauty Influencer / Makeup Artist

## Archetype
**Primary:** Influencer

**Secondary traits:** Perfectionist, image-obsessed, secretly insecure beneath the confidence

## The Fatal Flaw
**Core Flaw:** Vanity

**How it manifests:**
- Checks her reflection constantly, in any available surface
- Cannot walk past a mirror without looking
- Spends 3+ hours daily on appearance maintenance
- Defines her worth entirely through her appearance

**How it kills them:**
Tasha's obsessive mirror-gazing awakens an entity trapped in an antique mirror. The more she looks, the stronger her reflection becomes, until it has enough power to switch places with her, trapping her forever in the cold void behind the glass.

## Physical Appearance
- **Height/Build:** 5'9", model-thin, maintains her figure obsessively
- **Hair:** Platinum blonde (naturally dark brown), always perfectly styled
- **Distinguishing features:** High cheekbones (enhanced), full lips (filled), perfect skin (filters and procedures)
- **Typical outfit:** Designer or designer-adjacent, always camera-ready, refuses to wear the same outfit twice in photos

## Personality

### Positive Traits (that get twisted)
1. Genuinely skilled at makeup artistry - becomes obsession with surface-level transformation
2. Hardworking and disciplined - becomes compulsive control over appearance

### Negative Traits (that seal their fate)
1. Pathological need for validation - leads to constant mirror-checking
2. Inability to see beyond surfaces - leads to missing the warning signs

### Pet Peeves
- Bad lighting
- Unflattering angles
- Being photographed without permission
- People who "let themselves go"

### Fears
- Aging (terrified of turning 25)
- Being seen without makeup
- Becoming invisible, unremarkable

## Background

### Origin Story
Born in Moscow to a former model mother who made it clear that beauty was currency. Moved to Toronto at 8 when her parents divorced. Her mother's constant criticism ("your nose is too wide," "you're getting fat") created both her skill with makeup and her crippling insecurity. Started her beauty channel at 16, went viral at 19 with a "Russian beauty secrets" series. Has never felt pretty enough, despite millions of followers telling her otherwise.

### Why They Travel
Building content. Her European "Beauty Across Cultures" series. Also running from the Toronto influencer scene where younger creators are threatening her position.

### How They Ended Up at This Hostel
Booked "The Countess Hostel" in Prague specifically for its gothic aesthetic - perfect backdrop for her Halloween content. The antique mirror in her private room was advertised as a feature, not a warning.

## Voice & Speech

### Vocabulary
- Uses words like: "gorgeous," "stunning," "obsessed," "literally dying," "snatched," "beat" (makeup term)
- Avoids words like: "old," "tired," "natural," "makeup-free"
- Catchphrases: "Beauty is pain, but pain is temporary," "We're snatching wigs today," "Looking like a snack and feeling like a meal"

### Speech Pattern
- Upbeat and performative when camera is on
- Quieter, more critical when alone
- Constant self-commentary ("ugh, my pores")
- Talks to her reflection

### Internal Monologue Style
Relentlessly self-critical. Notices every flaw. Compares herself to everyone. A constant ticker of beauty assessments running through her head.

## Digital Presence

### Social Media
- **Platform of choice:** Instagram and YouTube (considering TikTok but fears the young audience)
- **Follower count:** 2.4 million on Instagram, 890K on YouTube
- **Posting style:** Perfectly curated, heavily edited, aspirational luxury aesthetic
- **Last post before death:** A mirror selfie in the antique mirror, caption: "Found the most stunning mirror in Prague! Giving very dark academia vampire queen vibes. What should I film here?"

### Online vs Reality
Posts about self-love and confidence while spending hours in self-critical spiral. Shows "no makeup" looks that require 45 minutes of "natural" makeup. Her followers think she's confident; she's terrified every single day.

## Relationships

### Family
- Mother still critiques her appearance over FaceTime
- Father remarried, barely in contact
- No siblings

### Friends
- Other influencers who are really competitors
- No one she trusts enough to show her real face

### Romantic
- Series of relationships with men who loved her image, not her
- Currently single after a photographer boyfriend sold unflattering candids to a tabloid

### At the Hostel
- Initially dismissive of the "normal" travelers
- Briefly bonds with a makeup-free backpacker who seems genuinely happy
- Avoids the hostel's communal bathrooms because the lighting is bad

## The Day They Die

### Morning
Wakes at 6 AM for her skincare routine. Spends an hour in front of the mirror. Notices her reflection seems to move slightly wrong but dismisses it as jet lag. Shoots content in her room, drawn repeatedly to the antique mirror.

### Their Last Normal Moment
Getting a genuine compliment from the backpacker who says she looks pretty without trying. For one moment, Tasha almost believes it.

### Final Thoughts
*That's not me. That's not me. Why is she smiling? I'm not smiling. Why can I feel the glass? Why is it so cold? No one will find me here. No one will even know I'm gone. She'll take my life and everyone will think she's me and no one will notice that I'm--*

### Last Words
"You're not me. You're not--" (cut off as she's pulled through)

## Writing This Character

### POV Notes
When in this character's head:
- They notice: Reflective surfaces, lighting quality, other people's skin/hair/bodies
- They ignore: Non-appearance-related details, emotional cues, genuine kindness
- They judge: Everyone on their appearance, constantly ranking
