# Book Identity

## Core Information

**Book Number:** 073
**Title:** Haunted Mirror
**Slug:** haunted-mirror
**Category:** Supernatural/Bizarre

## The Death (One Sentence)
A vain beauty influencer discovers an antique mirror in her hostel room that shows an increasingly malevolent reflection, until the reflection reaches through and pulls her into the mirror world where she suffocates in the cold void behind the glass.

## Tagline
"The reflection always wins."

## Themes
1. Vanity as literal destruction - the obsession with self-image consuming the self
2. The gap between curated appearance and inner reality
3. Doppelgangers and the fear of being replaced by a "better" version of yourself

## Tone Balance
- Comedy: 30%
- Horror: 50%
- Satire: 20%

## Target Reader
Anyone who spends too much time looking at themselves in mirrors or cameras. Beauty influencers, selfie-obsessed travelers, people who check their reflection in every surface. Those who fear aging, imperfection, or being seen as less than their best.

## Comparison Titles
- "Black Swan" meets "Oculus" in a gothic hostel
- "The Picture of Dorian Gray" meets "Mirrors"

## Content Warnings
- Body horror (mirror-based)
- Suffocation
- Psychological horror
- Doppelgangers
- Self-harm imagery (reflection attacking self)
- Beauty standards and vanity
