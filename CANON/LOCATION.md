# Location Profile

## The Hostel

**Name:** The Countess Hostel (Hostel Hrabenka)
**Type:** Boutique / Gothic / Historic
**Star Rating:** 4.6 on booking sites (praised for "atmosphere")
**Price Range:** 45-90 EUR per night

### History
Originally the townhouse of Countess Isabela Cernin, a notorious beauty of 18th century Prague society who vanished in 1789 at age 50 - though witnesses claimed she looked no older than 25. The house passed through various owners, each reporting strange occurrences with the mirrors. Converted to a boutique hostel in 2015 by new owners who "lean into" the gothic aesthetic. The antique mirror in Room 7 is from the original house - no one knows why it's never been moved.

### Architecture
- **Style:** Baroque townhouse, dark wood and ornate plasterwork
- **Floors:** 4 (ground floor common areas, 3 floors of rooms)
- **Total beds:** 28 (smaller, more intimate)
- **Notable features:** Original mirrors throughout (some antique, some new), candlelit hallways after 10 PM, a portrait of the Countess in the common room that "follows you with its eyes"

### Layout
```
Ground Floor:
- Reception (original entry hall, marble floors)
- Common room (former parlor, fireplace, the portrait)
- Small cafe/bar (former dining room)
- Kitchen (guests allowed limited access)

First Floor:
- Private rooms 1-4 (former bedchambers)
- Shared bathroom (modernized, multiple mirrors)

Second Floor:
- Private rooms 5-8 (Room 7 is the death room)
- Another shared bathroom

Third Floor:
- 8-bed mixed dorm (former servants' quarters)
- 6-bed female dorm
- Private bathroom for dorm guests

Basement (off-limits):
- Storage
- The "sealed room" that housekeeping won't enter
```

### The Death Location (Specific)
**Where exactly does the death occur?**
Room 7, second floor - the former dressing room of Countess Isabela. A small private room with a canopy bed, heavy velvet curtains (blood red), and the antique mirror. The mirror is 6 feet tall, gilded frame with cherubs and skulls intertwined, glass that seems to have a slight blue tint. The mirror is positioned so it's the first thing you see when you wake up. Guests have complained about this; management refuses to move it.

## The City

**City:** Prague
**Country:** Czech Republic
**Population:** ~1.3 million
**Known for:** Gothic architecture, astronomical clock, beer culture, reputation as one of the most beautiful and haunted cities in Europe

### Geography
- **Climate:** Continental, cold winters, mild summers
- **Terrain:** River valley, hills, cobblestoned streets
- **Weather during story:** Late October, cold fog, the kind of weather that makes everything look like a gothic novel

### Culture
- **Language(s):** Czech, English widely spoken in tourist areas
- **Religion(s):** Secular majority, Catholic heritage
- **Customs relevant to story:** Czech folklore is rich with supernatural elements - vodnik (water spirits), polednice (noon witch), and beliefs about mirrors showing the soul

### Tourist Scene
- **Backpacker popularity:** Very high - Prague is a classic backpacker destination
- **Type of tourists:** Mix of party tourists and culture seekers, Instagram-friendly architecture
- **Danger level:** Safe (per guidebooks - pickpockets are the biggest concern)

## Sensory Environment

### Sounds
- Morning: Church bells, tram bells, pigeons, the creak of old wood
- Afternoon: Tourist crowds in the distance, classical music from street performers
- Night: Eerie silence in the hostel, floors that creak when no one is walking, whispers that might be drafts
- Unique sounds: The mirrors seem to hum at certain frequencies

### Smells
- Hostel: Old wood, candle wax, faint perfume that no one is wearing (the Countess's scent)
- Street: Trdelnik (chimney cake), beer, the Vltava river
- Specific to death location: Room 7 smells like roses and something underneath, like cold stone

### Textures
- Surfaces: Velvet curtains, cool marble, warm wood panels, cold glass
- Climate feel: Drafty despite heating, pockets of inexplicable cold

### Tastes
- Local food: Svickova, goulash, dumplings, excellent beer
- Hostel offerings: Continental breakfast, the bar serves absinthe and local wines

### Visual Details
- Colors: Deep reds, golds, dark wood, candle flames
- Light quality: Dim and atmospheric, everything designed for shadows
- Distinctive visuals: Mirrors everywhere - more than any normal building needs, reflecting reflections reflecting reflections

Karolina knows something is wrong with Room 7. Mrs. Horakova has seen things she refuses to discuss. The owners acquired the property cheaply because of its "reputation" and don't care what happens as long as it stays profitable.

## Hostel Atmosphere

### Day Vibe
Quirky and Instagram-worthy. Guests pose with the Countess portrait, take selfies in the ornate mirrors. The gothic theme feels fun in daylight.

### Night Vibe
Different. The candles cast moving shadows. The mirrors seem darker. Guests report feeling watched. Some check out early. Some never check out at all.

### Guest Demographics
Influencers seeking aesthetic content, gothic enthusiasts, couples on romantic getaways, solo travelers drawn to the spooky reputation.

### Reputation
Reviews are polarized: half say it's the best hostel experience they've ever had, half describe nightmares and early departures. A handful of reviews just say "don't stay in Room 7" with no explanation.

## Practical Details

### Getting There
- Metro to Staromestska, 5-minute walk through winding streets
- The hostel is easy to miss - the door is unmarked, just a small plaque with a mirror symbol

### Check-in Process
Guests sign a "liability waiver" that mentions "historical preservation" and "paranormal experiences are not guaranteed or refundable." It's treated as a joke. It isn't.

### Rules
- No photography in the hallways after midnight (flash "disturbs the other guests")
- Mirror cleaning is staff-only
- Report any "unusual reflections" to reception
- Room 7 guests must confirm they've read and understood the "heritage room guidelines"
